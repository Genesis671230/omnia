# RAMZA sales system

Source: a 2-hour podcast on building a two-offer service business to 50 crore/year
(offer → distribution → sales), adapted to RAMZA. The principle that matters most:
**most "sales problems" are offer or distribution problems.** Fix the offer, then the
funnel, and only then the call.

Read in this order:
1. `offer-stack.md` — the 8 parts, already filled in for RAMZA (Clean Close).
2. `content-map.md` — how each content format carries the system, with a scorecard.
3. `gates-of-hell.md` — the discovery call script (WhatsApp / Zoom), objections, the 1–10 close.
4. `funnel.md` — the leak metrics, the 5-minute first touch, and the price-raise rule.

## Hard rules (on top of CLAUDE.md "RAMZA hard rules")
- Every figure (dates, penalties, prices, trial and setup terms) comes from `config/rules.json`.
  `null` prices → "Get a quote". `VERIFY` keys → flag to the founder before paid ads.
- **Never mention Omnia** in any video, post, ad, email or blog (founder rule 2026-10-24). Proof from
  our own books is "a Dubai e-commerce brand RAMZA was built inside" — true, and it names no one.
- **Urgency must be true**: a real deadline from rules.json, or a real capacity limit the founder has
  set. Never invent "only 3 slots left".
- **Belief-breaking claims need proof on the next beat.** No proof, no claim.
- **Never a guarantee or refund promise.** The risk reducer is the free setup in
  `offer.free_trial.free_setup`, with its conditions (skin in the game).
- Meta-safe: call out the audience ("UAE Shopify founders with 3+ gateways…"), never assert a personal
  attribute ("Are you losing money?").
- Not a registered tax agent, not an ASP — the disclaimer goes on every CTA.

## The loop (evolving)
After each piece ships, log in `../ramza-director/feedback-log.md`: hook used, gate it served, views or
leads, what we change next. Re-read the last 10 entries before writing new copy. The offer stack is
reviewed monthly with the `funnel.md` numbers: if more than 50% of calls close, prices are too low.
