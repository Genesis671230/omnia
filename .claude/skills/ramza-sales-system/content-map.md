# How every piece of content carries the sales system

Content is distribution. Each piece does one gate's work in public, so the call is short.

| Format | Job in the funnel | Required beats |
|---|---|---|
| Static ad / lead card | Specific callout → form | Audience callout · obstacle · outcome · "Free setup" · form CTA |
| Carousel | Gate 1 in public (need) | Belief-breaking hook · obstacle as rows · mechanism · Clean Close steps · proof · keyword CTA |
| Reel (15–35 s) | Hook + proof | 0–2 s belief-breaking line + visual object · proof by 6 s · one method beat · true urgency · keyword CTA |
| Explainer (40–60 s) | Method pitch | Question → proof → method (named) → process → offer (free setup) → CTA |
| Long video / blog | Trust (organic presence) | Specific outcome title · obstacles as H2s · proof screens · method · FAQ (objections from gates-of-hell) · CTA |
| Email / WhatsApp follow-up | Nurture vs hot | Hot: decision + next call. Nurture: a proof story, no pressure |
| Offer page | Close | Outcome · value stack vs obstacles · proof · free setup terms · price or "Get a quote" · disclaimer |

## Scorecard (the sales-director agent fills this for every piece; ship at ≥ 8/10)
1. Audience called out specifically (not a personal attribute)? 
2. One specific outcome with a number or date from rules.json/facts.json?
3. Hook = a belief-breaking statement or question?
4. Proof within the next beat (≤ 3 s in video)?
5. An obstacle named, and the stack item that kills it?
6. Method named (Clean Close) at least once in pitches?
7. Urgency true and sourced?
8. Risk reducer = free setup with its conditions, no guarantee language?
9. One CTA (keyword or form), with the disclaimer?
10. No Omnia name, no FTA/EmaraTax/MoF branding, no invented numbers?
