# Reel: "The Interview" (2026-10-20)

## Ideation: three concepts considered
1. **The Org Chart.** Top-down onto an office floor where the org chart is taped on the carpet; every line runs to the owner; RAMZA redraws it. Strong metaphor, but static, and the people are props.
2. **Lost & Found.** A drawer of unexplained bank lines, each a parcel nobody claims. Good pain, but it only sells reconciliation and misses the accountant angle.
3. **The Interview (chosen).** "We're hiring a Head of Operations. The candidate isn't human." Noor runs a hiring panel in a real Dubai office. The overworked ops employee says what eats his week, the RAMZA agent "interviews" by doing the job on the meeting-room screen, and the **accountant sits on the panel and approves**. That's the referral angle: RAMZA takes operations off the accountant's desk too, and they keep the finance work (VAT, Corporate Tax, sign-off).

**Why it sells:** the hiring frame is instantly understood by every business owner; "isn't human" is the curiosity gap; the pain is shown through a person, not a statistic; and the accountant's approval removes the objection "my accountant does this already."

**Positioning line:** *RAMZA runs your operations. Your accountant runs your finance.*

**Offer:** hire RAMZA for 7 days, free (rules.json offer.free_trial) + free e-invoicing readiness check. Accountants: send a client's messy month; the client gets the 7 days free.
